About Me

I'm a Data Analytics professional and aspiring Full-Stack Developer based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. I hold a B.Sc. in Applied Statistics and a Diploma in Computer Studies & Management, and I currently work as a Data Analytics Assistant at a leading multi-branch healthcare group(Lily Hospitals Ltd).

My analytical work spans healthcare performance reporting across 7+ branches — producing executive-level narrative reports that go beyond numbers to explain why metrics move, not just what they are. That skill — turning data into a story management can act on — is what makes me different.

On the development side, I'm actively building full-stack skills through a structured 18-month roadmap covering HTML/CSS, Python/Django, and DevOps. My live project, Augere Rentals, is a Django-based rental property web application.

I also offer Virtual Assistant services — combining administrative precision, technical support expertise, and strong written communication for remote clients worldwide.
